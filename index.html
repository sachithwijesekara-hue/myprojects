Background
Current State

Browns Hotels & Resorts manages 14 legal entities with a total workforce of 1,050 employees. Each hotel has a dedicated HR Partner reporting functionally to the Head of HR (Corporate) and administratively to the respective GM/Resident Manager.

Hotels acquired from Hemas currently run a separate, detached HRIS that is no longer functioning effectively — it now operates only as a firewall separation, with the vendor agreement inactive. This requires significant manual intervention for day-to-day HR operations, leading to excessive paperwork, duplication of effort, and increased man-hours.

Initial Requirement Submission
Recruitment module requirements formally handed over to hSenid on 16 July 2025 via support ticket #11394 · 129004-|-Recruitment module requirements. This was the trigger point for vendor development engagement.
Development Delay — hSenid
Development & UAT Build was planned for delivery by 30 Apr 2026. hSenid delivered on 24 Jun 2026 — approximately 55 days late. UAT commenced immediately upon receipt. HRIS reported issues on 2 Jul, 6 Jul, 8 Jul, 15 Jul, and 20 Jul. First joint UAT session with business users was held on 23 Jul — additional gaps and functional issues were identified. hSenid is currently addressing these prior to the second UAT round.
